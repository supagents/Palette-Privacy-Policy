Palette

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Palette is a browser extension that extracts color palettes from images, video frames, and screen regions you choose, and lets you save, organize, and export them.

The short version

Palette does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with anyone. Everything it stores lives only in your own browser, on your own device. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no third parties involved at any point.

What Palette accesses, and why

  • Images, video frames, and page content you explicitly interact with. When you right-click an image or video and choose an extraction option, or use the region-crop tool, or use the eyedropper, Palette reads the pixels of that specific content to compute a color palette. It does not read, scan, or process anything on a page you haven't explicitly acted on.
  • Broad host permission (<all_urls>). Palette requests access to all websites because its core feature — right-click any image, on any site, to extract its colors — only works if the extension can run on any page you visit. It does not use this access to monitor browsing, collect history, or read page content in the background; it activates only when you invoke one of its features.

Where your data is stored

  • Palette names, hex codes, notes, and collection organization are stored using the browser's built-in chrome.storage.local API.
  • Saved source images are stored using the browser's built-in IndexedDB API.

Both are local to your browser profile on your device. Palette has no backend server, and nothing you save is ever uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the extension removes this data (per Chrome's standard extension-storage behavior).

Sharing and exporting

Palette includes an optional "Export board as JSON" feature. This creates a file on your own device that you choose to save, share, or transfer — Palette itself never sends this data anywhere. The same applies to any other export (CSS, Tailwind, ASE, ACO, images) — all are local file downloads you initiate.

Third parties

Palette does not integrate with, send data to, or share data with any third-party service, analytics provider, or advertising network.
